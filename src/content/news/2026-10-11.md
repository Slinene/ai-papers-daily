## 10. NVIDIA Nemotron 微调后在 IOI/IMO 获金牌级成绩

- [One Model Family, Two Gold-Level Results: Fine-Tuning Nemotron for IOI and IMO](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/nemotron-ioi-and-imo-2026) `blog:hf-blog`
